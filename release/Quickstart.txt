WELCOME TO HOMEBOARD

1. Run the Homeboard installer, then open Homeboard from the finish screen or
   your Start menu. Python, Docker and a command prompt are not needed.
2. Follow the first-time setup: create your owner login, name your household,
   choose local time and appearance, and add the people at home.
3. Save your recovery kit somewhere private and separate from this PC. It can
   restore owner access without relying on email or a vendor account.
4. Use separate adult accounts on personal devices. Use a Display account for
   a kitchen screen; that account cannot change your plans or lists.

EVERYDAY USE

Open Homeboard to see today and the coming week. Edit buttons change existing
plans, tasks and shopping items. For a recurring event, choose whether your edit
affects one occurrence or the whole series. Weekly events can select weekdays.

The PC running Homeboard must remain powered on, signed in and awake for phones
and the kitchen display to reach it. Closing the browser does not stop the local
server. Use Start menu > Homeboard > Stop Homeboard when you want to stop it.

CONNECTING ANOTHER DEVICE

Devices need to be on the same home network. Sharing depends on private-network
firewall access and a trusted HTTPS setup. A guest Wi-Fi network may block access.
The seller's installation guide must cover the exact supported network setup.
This candidate does not create public internet access or a remote-access tunnel.

YOUR DATA

Application: %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\Homeboard
Data/configuration/logs: %LOCALAPPDATA%\Homeboard
Database snapshots: %LOCALAPPDATA%\Homeboard\data\backups

Updates replace the application, not your household data. Ordinary uninstall
keeps your data and backups so reinstalling can reconnect to the same household.
Export a household backup and copy database snapshots to another physical drive.
