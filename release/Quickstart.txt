WELCOME TO HOMEBOARD — FREE & OPEN SOURCE (MIT)

Windows: run Setup, then open Homeboard.
macOS: choose Apple Silicon/Intel DMG, drag the app to Applications, open it.
Linux: extract the x64/arm64 archive, then run: sh install.sh

Native packages bundle the runtime. No payment, activation or vendor account.
These are unsigned previews; macOS is not Apple-notarized. Keep normal OS
security protections enabled. Source/Docker alternatives are documented online.

FIRST USE
Follow the welcome flow, create your owner account and save the recovery kit.
Add family profiles, a plan and a few groceries. Adult sign-ins can edit;
a separate Display account is for the kitchen screen's /display page.

CONNECT PHONES AND TV
Open Help > Connect your devices for the home-network address. The hosting
computer must stay on, awake and signed in. Devices must be able to reach it.
Guest Wi-Fi can isolate devices. Remote access is not included.

YOUR DATA IS SEPARATE FROM THE APP
Windows: %LOCALAPPDATA%\Homeboard
macOS: ~/Library/Application Support/Homeboard
Linux: $XDG_DATA_HOME/homeboard (normally ~/.local/share/homeboard)
The database is data/family.sqlite3; backups are in data/backups.
Keep a copy of backups on another drive. Never share recovery codes.
